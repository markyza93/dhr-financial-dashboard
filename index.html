Month Revenue Expenses Profit Margin Land CapEx Equip. CapEx Taxes Total Add-backs EBITDA Proxy EBITDA Proxy Margin

Margin = Net Profit ÷ Revenue — the share of each revenue dollar retained after all costs. Add-backs = Land CapEx (land acquisitions) + Equipment CapEx (Mar 2026: filament extruder + 200× Bambu Lab P2S 3D printers, $601K — capitalised assets, not consumables) + Taxes paid in the month. All three are expensed in Total Expenses but are non-recurring or capital in nature, so they are added back to approximate recurring operating cash generation. EBITDA Proxy = Profit + Add-backs — a simplified proxy for recurring operating cash generation. EBITDA Proxy Margin = EBITDA Proxy ÷ Revenue, which normalises across months (e.g. Mar margin of −127% is distorted by $467K of land plus $601K of capitalised equipment; stripping both out turns Mar from −127% into an EBITDA Proxy Margin of +35%).
